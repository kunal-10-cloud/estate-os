# Estate OS: Agent and Team CRM Feature Benchmark

> **Scope:** the front office for agents and teams: lead capture, routing, speed-to-lead, contacts and households, smart lists, action plans and automations, tasks and calendar, pipelines, unified inbox, calling and dialer, compliance (TCPA, DNC, 10DLC), texting, email, WhatsApp, video, AI ISA (text and voice), AI copilot, lead scoring, website behavior tracking, reporting, team management, accountability and coaching, the mobile app and integrations.
>
> **Method (2026-10-08):** web search was exhausted and Brave began showing a CAPTCHA, which we did not bypass, so we used three other routes:
> - **Public help-center exports.** We pulled the public Zendesk Help Center APIs for Follow Up Boss (712 articles), Lofty (818) and Ylopo (431). We crawled sitemaps for BoldTrail/kvCORE (354 Intercom articles, re-fetching the ones that returned errors), Sierra Interactive (280), Real Geeks (264) and Rechat (393). That gives about 3,250 help pages indexed locally and searched by keyword, with about 130 of them read in full.
> - **Direct fetches** of CINC help (5 pages), FUB API docs (2), Structurely/21X (2), Wise Agent (3) and the Federal Register (2).
> - **The iTunes Search and Reviews APIs** for 11 apps.
>
> Every row cites the page we read. Anything we could not confirm from a fetched page is listed only under **Not verified**.
>
> **Vendor key:** FUB = Follow Up Boss (Zillow), BT = BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE, Inside Real Estate), SI = Sierra Interactive, RG = Real Geeks, WA = Wise Agent.
>
> **Priority key:** Must = needed for v1 parity with what FUB, Lofty and BT ship. Should = v1.x feature that sets us apart or matters to teams. Later = roadmap.

## 2. Lead routing and distribution

**Concrete rule model** (a union of the FUB, Lofty, SI and BT models):

| Part | Options |
|---|---|
| Scope | Company, office/team or personal. Lofty and BT route per entity, and **the rule set used depends on which entity owns the lead**. |
| Conditions | Source; tag (exact, or "empty or exact"); lead type (buyer or seller); city, county, ZIP, neighborhood or MLS area; price min/max; MLS number; phone number or area code; property type or site. |
| Condition logic | Rule-level ALL/ANY (FUB). Within a type, values combine with OR; across types they combine with AND (SI). |
| Working hours | Per-rule days and times (Lofty, SI). A lead outside the window falls to the next rule. |
| Distribution types | Round robin (with allocation weights); Next Up / weighted "hunger"; Blast Alert / First-to-Claim (push or simultaneous call); claim with timeout (sequential offer); Instant Call (dial agents, press 1); assign directly to a pond; direct assignment to a specific agent. |
| Fallback | Every rule needs a fallback, and the list ends in a default or catch-all rule that cannot be deleted. |
| On-assign actions | Tag, action plan or automation, drip, listing alerts or market report, auto-responder email or text, initial text, AI ISA opt-in, lender assignment. |
| Evaluation | Top to bottom; first match wins. |

## Top 12 differentiators for Estate OS

1. **Native WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Messenger and RCS in the unified inbox, with routing, consent and AI on every channel.** FUB says these are only "being considered" ([Inbox Apps](https://help.followupboss.com/hc/en-us/articles/23459816940055-Inbox-Apps-Overview)). FUB also cannot call or text outside the US and Canada ([International](https://help.followupboss.com/hc/en-us/articles/10321054750487-International-Calling-and-Texting)). This is decisive in India, MENA and APAC.
2. **Built-in, explainable routing with an audit log.** One rule engine: conditions → working hours → weighted, claim or pond distribution → fallback → on-assign actions. It ships Lofty-style Y/N rule logs and a "simulate this lead" preview. Today that power is spread across FUB, Lofty, SI and RG.
3. **Idle reassignment and accountability out of the box.** RG and BT build these in, but FUB needs a tag-plus-automation workaround ([recipe](https://help.followupboss.com/hc/en-us/articles/9961386591895-Recipe-Automatically-reassign-lead-if-no-contact-attempts-have-been-made)). Ship them as first-class toggles, counting only manual contact.
4. **A consent ledger as a core data type.** For each phone number, channel and seller, store the consent status and its evidence. Detect revocation in free text. Gate regulated technology (Lofty's model) and apply quiet hours by the lead's time zone. Every rival bolts this on.
5. **AI ISA (text plus voice) with a supervision UI.** Show the dispositions, a takeover queue, an "Ask me first" mode for pipeline and stage changes, and a daily brief. Price it per enrolled lead, not per seat (Lofty quota model; Ylopo prices by database).
6. **An explainable score that blends intent and engagement.** Lofty's "Lead Analysis" combined with SI's tiered intent, plus manual-contact decay (BT). One score drives the lists, alerts and AI enrollment.
7. **Households as first-class records.** Relationships plus linked leads plus the ability to search by any member, which the FUB app reportedly cannot do. One household timeline across buy, sell and rent.
8. **Mobile parity.** Call lists, the dialer, automations and reporting on the phone; an iPad layout; no forced logout. Competitor ratings run from 1.59★ for BT up to 4.28★ for FUB, with recurring complaints about speed and missing features.
9. **Agentic copilot plus MCP on every tier.** Chat is free and Cowork-style actions are metered. MCP and the API come standard; Lofty and RG ship MCP, while BT limits it to eXp.
10. **Native post-NAR workflow objects.** A buyer-agreement status, its expiry and compensation fields on the contact and deal. SI handles this only with a tag-based recipe ([BAA workflow](https://help.sierrainteractive.com/helpcenter/set-up-a-buyers-agency-agreement-tracking-workflow)).
11. **Transparent bundling.** Ponds, AI score, call recording and the dialer included, with no $50-225/mo pond add-ons (Lofty) or hidden ISA tiers. Month-to-month, consistent with the churn findings in the market research.
12. **A template-performance flywheel.** FUB-style percentile scores applied to texts, emails, AI scripts and action-plan steps, with A/B tests and one-click "swap to top performer".

## UX patterns worth copying

- **Default smart lists that teach the method.** FUB ships its lists as three collections and names each list by the action to take ("Qualify: Needs Contact"). Each list's filter recipe is shown, so the logic is understandable and editable.
- **Reports phrased as questions** with tooltips that explain each number (FUB Agent Activity).
- **"Swipe loses it, tap claims it" on first-to-claim**, plus a "Send Test Notification" button under My Devices (FUB).
- **The routing rule as a card stack.** Criteria chips, a working-hours chip, an agent list with per-agent weights, a distribution dropdown, an "add automations" footer, insert-between "+" buttons, and an undeletable catch-all fixed at the bottom (SI, BT).
- **A Y/N rule-trace log for each routed lead** (Lofty).
- **One status pill for availability** (Lofty Dialer), and a single toggle in the user list to pause routing (SI).
- **Colour-coded number states** on the profile: opted-out numbers turn orange, opted-in turn blue (FUB).
- **A "Needs-you" approval list for AI-proposed changes**, with Automatic, Ask-first and Off modes (Lofty).
- **One-lead daily brief by SMS**: one recommended lead instead of a dashboard (Lofty).
- **A Closed folder that reopens when the lead replies** (FUB Inbox).
- **Natural-language filter box** at the top of the filter panel, with results saved as a tab (Lofty AOS).
- **Plan settings that keep automation safe**: auto-pause on reply (but not on calls under 2.5 minutes), blackout dates and the actionable window, all set at plan level (FUB, SI).
- **Prebuilt digital-employee personas** with first-person pitches and skill tags on marketplace cards (Lofty).
