# Buyer customer portal: feature spec from first search to after closing

Research date: Oct 8, 2026. Scope: the buyer-facing portal in Estate OS. This is the screen set a buyer (and co-buyer) uses to find a home, get ready, tour, offer, close, and own. It is separate from the agent CRM, the transaction back office and the seller tools covered in [01-market-research.md](../01-market-research.md). That file settled the market and pricing questions. It also left two items open: whether any vendor ships a buyer-agreement gate before tours, and whether any vendor ships a settlement-native deal file. This document answers part of the first one (see D4).

**Method.** About 140 page reads across help centers, product pages, investor press releases, App Store descriptions and App Store review feeds. The reads covered Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Trulia, Compass, Opendoor, Offerpad, Rocket Mortgage, Zillow Home Loans, Better, Blend, Qualia, CertifID, Earnnest, HomeGauge, Spectora, Matic, HomeWiseDocs, Homebot, Knock, HomeLight, Flyhomes, Homeward, Landis, OneHome (Cotality), Rightmove, Zoopla, Domain, realestate.com.au, Property Finder, Bayut, 99acres, Magicbricks and NoBroker. Zillow.com, Redfin's newsroom, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Bayut and SkySlope blocked headless access with bot checks, so those vendors are cited from their own help centers, investor releases or App Store listings.

**Priority key.** **Must** = needed for a credible v1 that an agent would give to a client. **Should** = within the first year; expected by buyers who use Zillow or Redfin. **Later** = a differentiator or a regional pack that can wait.

## What the benchmarks say, in five lines

1. **The "one hub from search to keys" race started in 2026.** Zillow launched a personalized buyer hub on June 23, 2026. It has four milestones (budget, find, offer, close) that advance on their own: pre-approval moves the hub forward, and going under contract brings up closing tasks ([Zillow, Jun 2026](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Zillow-launches-a-personalized-hub-that-guides-home-buyers-from-first-search-to-closing/default.aspx)). Compass One covers the same arc for Compass clients ([Compass One](https://one.compass.com/)). Every other benchmark covers one stage.
2. **Co-buying is now a first-class object.** More than 60% of buyers co-shop ([Zillow messaging, Oct 2025](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2025/Zillow-launches-messaging-a-new-way-for-home-shoppers-to-collaborate-within-the-Zillow-app/default.aspx)). Zillow shipped shared collections and co-shopper chat. Domain still limits a merged shortlist to one partner ([Domain help](https://help.domain.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/360017890794-Merge-your-property-shortlist-with-other-property-seekers)).
3. **Financing is being wired into search.** Zillow labels every listing as inside or outside the buyer's Verified Pre-approval, including taxes, insurance, HOA and closing costs ([Zillow, Jun 2026](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Zillow-launches-a-personalized-hub-that-guides-home-buyers-from-first-search-to-closing/default.aspx)). Redfin carries one payment setting across every listing ([Redfin help](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021493471-Payment-Calculator)). Realtor.com lets buyers search by monthly payment ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/realtor-com-real-estate-rent/id336698281)).
4. **The closing half is still fragmented and weak.** Redfin's Deal Room is desktop-only for sellers ([Redfin help](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018729592-Deal-Room-Troubleshooting)). Qualia Connect's buyer app is rated 2.5 stars on 22 ratings, and reviewers say there is "no option to create an account" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/qualia-connect/id1279573162)). Endpoint, the best-known buyer closing app, now runs inside First American Title and no longer operates on its own ([Endpoint](https://www.endpoint.com/)).
5. **Wire fraud and identity are the trust layer.** CertifID covers every inbound payment "from EMD to C2C" (earnest money to cash to close) and cites $20.8B lost to cybercrime ([CertifID](https://www.certifid.com/)). Qualia Shield verifies government ID, scores every wire and insures eligible wires up to $2M ([Qualia Shield](https://www.qualia.com/shield/)). Opendoor and Redfin require ID verification before a buyer can tour ([Opendoor](https://help.opendoor.com/property-access/self-guided-tours/identity-verification), [Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001454391-Pre-Approval-Upload)).

## Top 15 differentiators for Estate OS

These are ranked by how much they separate Estate OS from what a brokerage can assemble today from Zillow, OneHome, dotloop and Qualia. Each one sits on the shared Person / Household / Deal / Property graph from the market research, which single-stage tools cannot copy.

1. **One buyer hub across all four stages, owned by the agent's brand.** Zillow's hub (Jun 2026) is the only consumer version, and it routes to Zillow's own agents and loans. Compass One is limited to Compass. Estate OS gives any brokerage a white-label hub (R1, C1, C2, X8).
2. **Settlement-native agreement flow.** Touring agreement, then exclusive agreement, with a compensation card and a tour gate (R17–R20, T13). Zillow proves the pattern exists, but only for Premier Agents in listed states. Nobody ships it for independent brokerages.
3. **Shared workspace for many collaborators with roles.** Co-buyers plus agent plus guests (parent, attorney), with per-message visibility (D32–D37, X5, X6). Domain caps it at one partner, and Zillow's chat is buyer-to-buyer.
4. **Deal-level unified thread with channel bridging.** One conversation across buyer, agent, TC, lender and title, answerable from SMS or WhatsApp (X1, X2). Qualia does this only for title. Portals stop at the agent.
5. **Wire-safe money movement built in.** EMD by ACH, verified wiring instructions frozen in-portal, callback verification, scam reporting and a recovery flow (C12–C17). Today a buyer gets this only if their title company happens to buy CertifID or Shield.
6. **Budget labels on every listing, with any lender.** Zillow's version requires Zillow Home Loans. Estate OS accepts any lender's pre-approval upload (R8) and still labels listings by target and max budget (R4, D60). This is RESPA-friendly and lender-neutral.
7. **Plain-language offer builder with counter redlines.** Price, EMD, financing, contingencies, dates and concessions in a guided flow, with a "what this means" summary (O12) and redlined counters (O15), signable on a phone (O13). This fixes the top dotloop complaint.
8. **Typed document requests with save-as-you-go and magic links.** Directly addresses Qualia Connect's 2.5-star review themes (C5–C10).
9. **Inspection-to-repair-request pipeline.** Report items become an addendum list with co-editing and audit (C20, C21). HomeGauge offers this only to its own inspectors' clients.
10. **Mobile-first deal room.** Full deal room on the phone (C38). Redfin's owner dashboard is still desktop-only.
11. **Post-close homeowner mode created automatically from the deal.** No re-entry: value, equity, document archive, refi and move-up moments, referral routing (P1–P6, P10, P15). Homebot does this as a separate product with a separate database.
12. **Brokerage exclusive inventory and pre-market access in the portal.** Coming-soon and private-exclusive feeds with pre-book tour (D22–D24). This is the brokerage's reason for clients to use its portal rather than Zillow.
13. **Notification discipline.** Per-type, per-channel settings that stay set, and quiet hours (D18, X3, X4). This is the most repeated complaint across Zillow, Redfin and Realtor.com reviews.
14. **Regional packs on one core.** US settlement rules, UK material information, UAE verified listings with DLD transaction comps, India bank-loan marketplace and RERA, AU inspection planner and building reports. No incumbent spans more than one region well.
15. **Agent-side intelligence from consented activity.** What the client viewed, saved, shared and asked, which feeds CRM tasks (D42, X24). This closes the loop with the CRM module, the way Zillow Pro does for Follow Up Boss users.

## UX patterns worth copying

1. **"Two numbers" budget badge on every card.** Each listing shows whether it fits the buyer's comfortable target and their max qualification ([Zillow BuyAbility, Jul 2025](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2025/Summer-just-got-hotter-Zillow-debuts-five-powerful-new-features/default.aspx)). Copy it as a small pill on the result card: "In budget", "Stretch" or "Over".
2. **Set the payment once, apply it everywhere.** Redfin carries the logged-in buyer's down payment, rate, tax and insurance edits to every listing ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021493471-Payment-Calculator)).
3. **Milestones that move themselves.** Zillow's hub advances when the buyer is pre-approved and reveals closing tasks when they go under contract ([Zillow](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Zillow-launches-a-personalized-hub-that-guides-home-buyers-from-first-search-to-closing/default.aspx)). Buyers never see an empty checklist for a stage they are not in.
4. **Shortlist feeds the planner automatically.** Domain adds every shortlisted home's inspection times to the planner with a map view, and lets buyers rate each home after the visit ([Domain](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/domain-real-estate-property/id319908646)).
5. **Lightning-bolt slots.** Redfin marks slots where an agent is already free with a lightning bolt, so buyers pick the time most likely to confirm ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001432232-Scheduling-a-Tour)).
6. **"Yes, add this home" while a tour is open.** After booking one tour, every active listing shows a one-tap add-to-tour prompt ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001432232-Scheduling-a-Tour)).
7. **Honest "not confirmed yet" copy.** Zillow tells shoppers when a tour is not yet confirmed and that the agent will follow up with alternates ([Zillow](https://www.zillow.help/article/faq-take-a-tour-and-tour-connections-zd360019248553)).
8. **Sign now or sign at the door.** Zillow's touring agreement offers "Accept and Sign digitally or opt to sign at the home", and one signature covers every tour for seven days ([Zillow](https://www.zillow.help/article/touring-agreement-activation-zd31591616608531)).
9. **Typed uploads with share toggles.** Redfin's upload pop-up asks for a document type so the right department sees it first. "Other" expands to per-recipient toggles, and a Settings link under each file shows who can see it ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001432272-Upload-and-Download-Documents-from-the-Deal-Room-or-Owner-Dashboard)).
10. **Consent-first sharing of sensitive documents.** Redfin explains why the lender won't share the Closing Disclosure and gives the buyer a yes/no share toggle they can reverse ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004022651-How-to-Share-a-Closing-Disclosure)).
11. **Magic links.** Qualia lets parties finish a task from a link without logging in ([Qualia](https://www.qualia.com/connect-for-title-and-escrow/)). This is critical for co-buyers who never install the app.
12. **"You have N tasks remaining" plus a celebration header.** CertifID's closing screen pairs a task counter with "Congratulations, you're under contract" and title-company branding ([CertifID](https://www.certifid.com/)).
13. **Before/after slider for AI staging.** Zillow's staging icon on select photos opens style cards with refresh, a before/after slider, and an "empty the room" option ([Zillow](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2025/Zillow-brings-AI-powered-Virtual-Staging-to-Showcase-listings/default.aspx)).
14. **Comparative context on the listing.** Property Finder: "This property costs 38% more than the average… 17% bigger than the average", next to actual sold and rented comps from the land department ([Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/plp/buy/apartment-for-sale-dubai-downtown-dubai-burj-khalifa-area-burj-khalifa-154608004.html)).
15. **Keyword grammar.** Zoopla supports commas for OR, "quotes" for phrases and `-term` to exclude. It also states the caveat that "no garden" still matches "garden" ([Zoopla](https://help.zoopla.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/360006065138-How-can-I-search-for-sales-properties-using-keywords)). Estate OS can fix that caveat with negation detection.
16. **48-hour privacy hold on agent notes.** Redfin delays public tour comments for 48h so a client can make an offer first ([Redfin](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001432212-Tour-Insights)). Reuse this for any agent-authored listing commentary.
17. **AI that is allowed to say no.** Homebot AI tells homeowners "refinancing doesn't make sense for you right now" when that is true ([Homebot](https://homebot.ai/blog/q2-2026-product-releases)). This builds trust for the hand-off to the agent.
18. **Hidden-listings collection.** REA keeps hidden homes in a "Hidden" collection with Unhide, so hiding is never destructive ([REA](https://help.realestate.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360037604632-Hiding-listings-on-realestate-com-au)).
19. **Pre-market labels plus pre-book.** Zillow Preview listings are clearly labeled, filterable, and offer "pre-book a tour" ([Zillow](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Zillow-Preview-listings-are-now-live-on-Realtor-com-offering-the-same-early-access-on-the-two-most-visited-real-estate-platforms-in-the-country/default.aspx)).
20. **ID check once, reused.** Opendoor's ID-plus-selfie verification (Stripe Identity) is a one-time step and states the common failure reasons up front ([Opendoor](https://help.opendoor.com/property-access/self-guided-tours/identity-verification)).

## Failure evidence to design against (App Store reviews, 2023–2026)

- **Qualia Connect (2.5★, 22 ratings):** no sign-up path, an "organization name" required from buyers, no save-as-you-go, and the app and web logins disagree ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/qualia-connect/id1279573162)).
- **dotloop:** signing on a phone "took about an hour (needed two devices)"; clients "have to share it back to the group"; frequent logouts ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dotloop/id605565510)).
- **Zillow / Redfin / Realtor.com:** notification spam and settings that revert; redesigns that move the filters; saved homes lost on random sign-out ([Zillow](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zillow-real-estate-rentals/id310738695), [Redfin](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/redfin-buy-sell-rent-homes/id327962480), [Realtor.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/realtor-com-real-estate-rent/id336698281)).
- **Homes.com:** filters ignored, map boundaries removed, frequent sign-outs ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/homes-com-real-estate-rent/id306423353)).
- **Opendoor:** access codes that don't arrive, ID verification failing at the door, AI phone support loops, most homes "View Only" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/opendoor-homes-for-sale/id1143708068)).
