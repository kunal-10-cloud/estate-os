# Estate OS: Transactions and Brokerage Back Office Feature Benchmark

> **Scope:** everything from a signed agreement to a paid agent and closed books. That covers transaction files, forms libraries, e-signature, checklists and templates, compliance review and broker approval, timelines and AI contract reading, transaction-coordinator (TC) workflows, earnest money and trust accounting, commission plans, CDAs and disbursements, agent billing, payouts, 1099s, accounting integrations, financial reporting, retention and audit, offers, post-NAR buyer agreements and MLS status sync.
>
> **Method (2026-10-08):** web search was exhausted, and Brave showed a CAPTCHA after the first parallel batch. We did not bypass it. Instead we navigated the vendors' public help centers directly with a headless browser, and read about **270 pages** (raw text kept in the scratchpad):
> - **SkySlope Suite:** about 60 Freshdesk articles, including monthly product updates for 2025 and 2026.
> - **SkySlope Books** (formerly Zipi): about 30 articles.
> - **BoldTrail BackOffice** (formerly Brokermint): about 35 Intercom articles plus its release notes.
> - **Paperless Pipeline:** about 30 help articles plus its "What's New" page.
> - **ListedKit:** about 20 Intercom articles.
> - **Rechat:** 11 GitBook pages, read as markdown.
> - **Real Brokerage reZEN:** 16 Zendesk articles.
> - **Glide:** 10 Intercom articles.
> - **Lone Wolf:** product pages and the June–September 2026 release notes.
> - **Smaller vendors:** Open to Close pricing and features, Loft47, BrokerSumo, Trackxi, Transactly, Form Simplicity, Earnnest, ShowingTime and Qualia Connect.
> - **dotloop:** its public API v2 docs. The marketing site and help center are bot-blocked or behind a login.
> - **Rules and law:** the NAR settlement FAQ and consumer guide, California B&P §10148 and Florida §475.5015.
> - **App ratings:** the iTunes Search and Reviews APIs for 9 apps.
>
> Every row cites the page we read. Claims we could not confirm are listed only under **Not verified**.

## Top 12 differentiators for Estate OS

1. **One deal object from buyer agreement to agent paystub.** Today the transaction tool and the back office are separate products joined by one-time or one-way syncs:
   - SSB statuses must be updated by hand after import.
   - The QBO sync is one-time only.
   - SkySlope and Books EMD records are explicitly not connected.

   Estate OS should hold the Buyer Record → Deal → Checklist → CDA → Ledger → 1099 chain as one record ([SSB mapping](https://bookssupport.skyslope.com/support/solutions/articles/158000122019-skyslope-field-and-status-mapping), [SSB EMD note](https://bookssupport.skyslope.com/support/solutions/articles/158000121768-manage-earnest-money-collecting-deposits-new-deal-page-view)).
2. **The buyer agreement as a first-class object, with compensation enforced.** Copy REAL's Buyer Record (statuses, termination approval, one record per transaction). Then add what no one advertises: carry the agreed compensation into the CDA and warn when the total from all sources exceeds it.
3. **AI intake with field-level citations, offered by default.** Combine PP AI Create and Update ($1 per run, hover to see the source) with LK email intake, execution-date timeline rooting and dependency cascades. Set a per-role AI permission and a monthly spending cap from day one.
4. **AI audit for brokers in every state.** Cover signature and initial detection, cross-document date and price mismatches, Blocker/Warning/Info severity and pre-filled rejection comments. SkySlope's Smart Assist is limited to CA and AZ, so national coverage is an opening ([SS Quick Audit](https://support.skyslope.com/support/solutions/articles/156000366255-file-review-quick-audit)).
5. **Full e-signing and review on mobile.** The SS app has 4.83★ overall, but its written reviews say "No Digisign?" and "4.8 is not real." dotloop users can't fill assigned blanks on mobile. BTBO mobile can't record EMD. Full mobile parity is a clear wedge ([SS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1608944544), [DL](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id605565510)).
6. **Commission engine that handles mid-deal proration and explains itself.** Cover sliding scales on a selectable base, chained plans, cap carry-in, company-paid deductions and team award-allocation proration. Show agents a running "remaining to next tier," and include a celebratory cap-closing statement.
7. **Native earnest money and trust, with three-way reconciliation built in.** Combine digital EMD requests (Earnnest-style gated payments) with a per-property trust liability ledger and a monthly reconciliation worksheet that requires a $0.00 difference and broker sign-off. No more exporting to QuickBooks to prove trust compliance.
8. **Disbursement as a visible state machine with batch actions.** Status runs Incomplete → Pending Approval → Pending Payment Confirmation → Pending Remaining Payouts → Processed. Add bulk confirm, bulk ACH payouts (next day), reversible payouts, and the Expected / Billed / Paid / Balance finance view per deal.
9. **TC-native operations.** Include "act on behalf of" without shared logins, the TC as a contact type with a TC fee on the CDA, conditional mini-templates, scheduled and triggered messages, and AI email rules. Offer per-file pricing for TCs alongside seat pricing.
10. **Agent billing that doesn't fight agents.** Default to invoice-only, collect from commission, let agents self-pay, choose who pays processing fees, and run a statement cycle with a review window before send.
11. **Open data and transparent metering.** Provide free monthly open-format backups, APIs and webhooks, an MCP server, metered e-sign and AI with spending caps, and $15-style overages instead of forced plan upgrades. This addresses the lock-in complaints in the market report.
12. **Retention and audit enforced by state rules.** Apply retention rules per state (CA 3 years, FL 5 years plus 2 after litigation), keep rejected-offer storage, and generate an audit package on demand: envelope certificates, review log, trust reconciliations.
