# Estate OS: ecosystem services and user pain points (round 2)

**In short:** Estate OS should treat the services around a deal (mortgage, title, insurance, inspection, moving, seller financing) as **one tracked closing checklist** with built-in compliance, not as a list of partner ads. The reviews below agree on what goes wrong today:

- **Partner "concierge" products upsell.** Updater, the AppFolio moving concierge and Realtor.com/HomeLight lead forms all draw complaints about being sold extras or having contact details passed on.
- **Fees are hidden from tenants.** Resident Portal, FolioSpace and DoorLoop users report surprise convenience and processing fees.
- **Title and closing steps are hard to see.** Rocket, Zillow and eXp reviews describe closings stalled at the title company with nobody explaining why.
- **Wire fraud is rising and most consumers are unprepared.** The FBI counted $275.1M in losses in 2025, and 52% of consumers say they were not aware, or only somewhat aware, of the risk.
- **Brokers want one interface, not one more dashboard.** T3 Sixty puts adoption of brokerage-provided tech at 15-20%.

The regulatory picture has moved in two directions:

- **Looser:** the CFPB withdrew 67 guidance documents in May 2025, and FinCEN's residential real estate reporting rule was vacated by a court in March 2026.
- **Unchanged or tighter:** RESPA Section 8 and Regulation X still apply. The CFPB's 2023 opinion on mortgage comparison-shopping platforms was *not* withdrawn. The FTC opened rulemaking on rental junk fees in March 2026.

So the right design is "compliance by construction": fair rotation of providers, disclosure screens, flat fees for marketing only, and no pay-per-referral.

| **FinCEN Residential Real Estate Rule** | "On March 19, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Texas issued an order vacating the Residential Real Estate Rule … FinCEN … has appealed." Filers are not currently required to report. | [FinCEN RRE](https://www.fincen.gov/rre) |
| **Remote online notarization (RON)** | Legal in 49 states plus DC as of 2026; California's permanent law takes effect Jan 1, 2030. As of late 2025, 42 states let their own notaries perform RON. | [Proof](https://www.proof.com/blog/is-remote-online-notarization-legal-in-all-states) |
| **FTC rental fee rulemaking (ANPRM, Mar 13, 2026)** | The FTC is asking whether landlords hide the "true total rent", and the "nature, purpose, amount, refundability, optionality and recurrence" of fees. This affects tenant portals and move-in upsells. | [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-unfair-or-deceptive-rental-housing-fee) |

**Design rule that follows from these:** Estate OS takes no per-lead or per-closing pay from settlement providers.

- **Allowed revenue:** flat SaaS fees, plus marketing fees at fair market value when the provider really receives marketing.
- **Provider lists:** shown in a neutral order the consumer can sort.
- **Built-in compliance:** an ABA disclosure step and a "you are free to choose your own provider" screen are part of the product.
- **Product decision:** whether Estate OS itself becomes a licensed insurance agency, as Matic is, is a separate decision.

## Top 10 differentiators

1. **One interface, one record.** Shared person, household, property and deal objects across CRM, TM, back office, PM and concierge, answering T3 Sixty's "one user interface" ask directly (B1-B3).
2. **A closing command center visible to the consumer.** Every service (lender, title, inspection, appraisal, insurance, RON) appears as a step on one timeline with a named owner, status from integrations (Encompass EPC, title order status), and a risk engine for rate-lock, CTC and title slippage (A2, B14, B18).
3. **Wire-safe by default.** Wiring instructions never travel by email; payee identity is verified; fraud education fires automatically at each money step (A2, B19).
4. **RESPA-safe service marketplace.** Neutral sorting of providers, no pay-per-lead, flat or fair-market-value marketing fees, ABA disclosures signed in-app, Homebot-style co-marketing cost split ledger, and an audit export for regulators (A0, A1).
5. **True-cost math everywhere.** PITI plus insurance quote, HOA and maintenance reserve on listings and offers. A seller net sheet that compares price cut with concessions. A bridge vs cash-offer vs list comparison (A6, B15, B17).
6. **Fee transparency for tenants and landlords.** Total-rent-first checkout, a free ACH path, bring-your-own renters insurance, and a landlord-and-tenant fee ledger, ready for the FTC rental-fee rule (B25-B27).
7. **AI that does transaction work with citations and never blocks a human.** Contract-to-timeline, auto status updates and disclosure summaries, each with source clauses and an approve step. Forms always available; "talk to a person" always offered (B7, B22, B27).
8. **Data you own, pricing you can predict.** Export any time, 30-day soft-delete and restore, self-serve cancel, published prices with per-file pricing, and adoption analytics for brokers (B2, B4, B8, B10, B21).
9. **Consent-scoped lead handling and verified listings.** The consumer chooses one agent with no contact resale; listings carry a verified-owner badge; every message and listing has a one-tap scam report (B11, B29).
10. **A field-grade PM back office.** Multi-tier trust accounting, a work order → invoice → payment chain (reusing the field-services bundle), next-day payouts, and a mobile accounting approvals app (B23, B24, B30, B31).
